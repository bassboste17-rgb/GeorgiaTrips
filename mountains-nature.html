Mountains & Nature of Georgia

Georgia is a paradise for mountain lovers and nature enthusiasts. The Greater Caucasus range, with peaks exceeding 5,000 meters, forms a natural border in the north. From the glacial valleys of Svaneti to the alpine meadows of Tusheti, from the volcanic landscapes of Javakheti to the lush forests of Adjara, Georgia offers incredibly diverse natural landscapes. The country is home to numerous national parks, protected areas, and UNESCO-recognized natural heritage sites.

Top Mountain Destinations

Explore the most spectacular mountain destinations in Georgia

Kazbegi (Stepantsminda)

Home to Mount Kazbek (5,047m) and the iconic Gergeti Trinity Church. A paradise for mountaineers, hikers, and photographers seeking dramatic Caucasus views.

5,047m Gergeti Church Trekking

Svaneti (Mestia & Ushguli)

A UNESCO World Heritage region famous for its medieval defensive towers, ancient traditions, and the highest permanently inhabited settlement in Europe - Ushguli.

UNESCO Svan Towers Glaciers

Tusheti

One of the most remote and pristine regions of Georgia, accessible only by a dramatic mountain road from June to October. Ancient villages, unique traditions, and stunning alpine scenery.

Abano Pass Wildlife Camping

Borjomi-Kharagauli

One of the largest national parks in Europe, covering diverse ecosystems from lowland forests to alpine meadows. Famous for mineral springs, hiking trails, and rich biodiversity.

National Park Mineral Water Birdwatching

Lagodekhi Reserve

Georgia's oldest nature reserve, established in 1912. Home to pristine forests, waterfalls, alpine lakes, and diverse wildlife including Caucasian tur and brown bears.

Nature Reserve Waterfalls Endemic Species

Martvili & Okatse Canyon

Spectacular limestone canyons with emerald green waters. Boat rides through narrow gorges, suspended walkways, and breathtaking natural formations in western Georgia.

Boat Rides Photography Canyon Walks
