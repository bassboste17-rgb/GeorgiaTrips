Georgia's Black Sea Coast

Georgia's Black Sea coastline stretches over 310 kilometers, offering diverse coastal experiences from the vibrant resort city of Batumi to tranquil beaches in Ureki with unique magnetic black sand known for its therapeutic properties. The subtropical climate of Adjara provides lush vegetation, palm trees, and warm summers perfect for beach holidays. The coast also features ancient fortresses like Gonio and Petra, botanical gardens, and a rich maritime heritage dating back to the ancient Greek civilization.

Coastal Destinations

Explore the best destinations along Georgia's Black Sea coast

Batumi

The jewel of the Black Sea coast and the capital of Adjara. Batumi blends modern architecture with a charming Old Town, featuring the Batumi Boulevard, dancing fountains, the Alphabet Tower, and a vibrant nightlife. The city's subtropical gardens and botanical park are truly world-class.

Modern City Boulevard Nightlife

Kobuleti

A popular family-friendly resort town with a 10-kilometer-long beach. Known for its pine forests, therapeutic sea air, and a relaxed atmosphere. The Kobuleti Protected Areas offer unique wetlands and birdwatching opportunities along the coast.

Family Friendly Pine Forests 10 km

Ureki & Magnetic Sand

Famous worldwide for its unique magnetic black sand beaches with proven therapeutic properties. The naturally magnetized sand is believed to help treat cardiovascular, nervous system, and joint disorders. A truly one-of-a-kind beach experience.

Magnetic Sand Therapeutic Wellness

Gonio-Apsaros Fortress

An ancient Roman fortress dating back to the 1st-2nd century AD, located near the Turkish border. One of the oldest fortifications in Georgia, it combines history with nearby pristine beaches. According to legend, the burial site of the Apostle Matthias is within its walls.

Roman Fortress 2,000 Years Beach Nearby

Batumi Botanical Garden

One of the largest botanical gardens in the former Soviet Union, spanning 111 hectares on the hills above the Black Sea. Home to over 5,000 plant species from around the world, with stunning coastal views and diverse climate zones within the garden.

5,000+ Species Coastal Views 111 ha

Sarpi & Kvariati

The southernmost beach resorts of Georgia, near the Turkish border. Sarpi is famous for its impressive waterfall cascading directly into the sea, while Kvariati offers the cleanest and deepest waters on the Georgian coast, ideal for diving and snorkeling.

Waterfall Diving Clear Water
